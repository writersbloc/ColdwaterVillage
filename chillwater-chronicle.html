Cover the Mayor's Address

Chillwater, population 311 (plus yetis, who don't fill out census forms).

Three nights ago, under record snowfall, the Frostbite Bridge collapsed — the village's only route to the eastern valley. Tonight, Mayor Yeti is giving her first public address since the collapse, and you, the newest hire at the Chronicle, have been sent to cover it.

Your editor's only instruction before she hands you your notebook:

"Don't just print what she says. Read it critically."

Town Square, dusk

Mayor Yeti's Address

Read it once for understanding before you do anything else.

P1Neighbors of Chillwater — three nights ago, under record snowfall, the Frostbite Bridge gave way. For sixty years that bridge carried every wagon, every sled, every one of us to the eastern valley — longer than any bridge built in these mountains before or since.

P2I have walked the wreckage myself. I have spoken with builders. And I can tell you plainly: this village cannot rebuild the old way. The old way is why we lost it.

P3That is why, starting this spring, I am proposing a new toll bridge — modern, storm-proof, and paid for by a small fee on visitors passing through, not a single coin from your own pockets.

P4Some of you will say Chillwater has never needed a toll before. I say Chillwater has never needed one before because Chillwater has never lost a bridge before.

P5This is the responsible path. This is the only path. And I intend to see it built by first snowfall.

— Mayor Yeti, delivered from the Town Square steps

Reporter's notebook — step 1 & 2

Summarize, Then Paraphrase

A summary condenses the whole text in your own words. A paraphrase restates one part, at similar length, to clarify meaning — without smuggling in your own opinion.

Which is the best one-sentence summary of the whole speech?

The bridge broke, and it's nobody's fault — these things happen in the mountains.
After the Frostbite Bridge's collapse, Mayor Yeti proposes rebuilding it as a toll bridge funded by visitor fees.
Mayor Yeti gave a moving, honest speech about the tragedy that struck Chillwater.

Which best paraphrases P5 ("This is the responsible path. This is the only path.") without adding opinion?

The mayor admits there might be other reasonable options, but prefers this one.
The mayor states that this plan is the correct choice and presents no alternative as viable.
The mayor's plan is clearly the only sensible option for Chillwater.
Reporter's notebook — step 3

Fact, Inference, or Opinion?

Every claim in an argument rests on one of three things: a verifiable fact, a credible inference drawn from evidence, or an unsubstantiated opinion. Tag each line from the speech.
